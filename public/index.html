Questions

Before you start

Do I need a fully formed business idea?

No. A rough idea is enough. Part of the diagnostic's job is to show you which version of your idea, if any, is worth pursuing.

How long does it take?

Most people finish in 20 to 30 minutes. Some questions ask you to describe things in your own words, and the more specific you are, the more specific your report will be.

Is the report really personalised?

Yes. The verdict, risks, business model and priorities are driven by your answers and how they combine. Two people with the same overall score can receive completely different recommendations.

How does this relate to The Encore Programme™?

The diagnostic tells you what to do. The Encore Programme is the 12-week programme that helps you do it. If you join within 14 days of your report, your £397 is credited in full.

Is this financial advice?

No. The diagnostic assesses the commercial readiness of a business idea. It doesn't give financial, legal or investment advice, and it won't recommend pension, borrowing or investment decisions.
